About Us

A Legacy of Excellence

Established in 1999, Sri Vidya College of Engineering has been at the forefront of technical education for over two decades. Our institution blends rigorous academics with hands-on experience.

With state-of-the-art laboratories, a rich library, and industry partnerships with top MNCs, we ensure every student graduates ready for the professional world.
