Who is eligible to participate in SSE Horizon '26?

Students currently enrolled in any recognized engineering, arts, science, or management college across India are eligible.

Will hostel accommodation be provided for outstation participants?

Yes, hostel accommodation and dining facilities are available on-campus upon prior request in the registration modal.

Is there on-spot registration available?

Online registration is strongly recommended as seats for high-demand events like HackSSE and Masterclasses are limited.
