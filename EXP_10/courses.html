Academic Offerings

We offer five core engineering disciplines leading to a Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) degree. The programs are structured over 4 years (divided into 8 semesters) following an industry-aligned curriculum model.

Course (B.Tech) Duration Intake Capacity Eligibility
Computer Science & Engineering 4 Years 120 Students 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Math (Min 60%)
Information Technology 4 Years 60 Students 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Math (Min 60%)
Electronics & Communication 4 Years 60 Students 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Math (Min 55%)
Electrical & Electronics 4 Years 60 Students 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Math (Min 55%)
Mechanical Engineering 4 Years 60 Students 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Math (Min 55%)

Admission Guidelines

Admission to all B.Tech seats is based on merit rankings in the national level engineering entrance examinations followed by counseling. A relaxation of 5% in marks is provided to candidates belonging to reserved categories under state guidelines.
